Build a sleek, single-page product launch website for the Fujifilm X-T10 digital camera. 
The design should be modern, immersive, and feature interactive 3D animations that allow the camera to be viewed from all angles.

Sections:
1. Hero Section:
   - Fullscreen 3D model of the Fujifilm X-T10 camera.
   - Interactive rotation: users can drag or scroll to rotate the camera 360° horizontally and vertically.
   - Headline: "100 Million Pixel HD Real Shot."
   - Subtext: "Dust Proof. Water Proof. Built for Adventure."
   - Launch date displayed prominently: "20.04.2021."
   - Call-to-action button: "Pre-Order Now."

2. Features Section:
   - As users scroll, the camera animates to highlight specific features (lens zoom, body durability, waterproof seals).
   - Smooth transitions between angles when showcasing each feature.
   - Text panels slide in with short descriptions.

3. Animation Details:
   - Camera spins slowly by default, pausing when hovered.
   - Zoom-in animation on lens when feature is selected.
   - Exploded view animation showing internal components (sensor, lens mount, battery).
   - Dust and water particle effects subtly animated in background.

4. Story Section:
   - Timeline animation: "Concept → Design → Engineering → Launch."
   - Each stage accompanied by animated camera angles and short text.

5. Call-to-Action Section:
   - Bold button: "Order Yours Today."
   - Button pulses gently with hover glow effect.
   - Contact info: website and phone number at bottom.

Design Style:
- Colors: dark background with neon accents (blue, silver, white).
- Typography: bold sans-serif for headlines, clean sans-serif for body text.
- Animations: smooth easing, lightweight WebGL/Three.js for 3D rendering.
- Responsive: optimized for desktop and mobile.
